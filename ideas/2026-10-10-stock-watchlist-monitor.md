## The idea

Put a wish list (watchlist) of stocks in the Robinhood account: stocks I want to invest in but haven't bought yet. Then set up a scheduled task with a lot of monitors that checks them on a schedule and sends me data about them. I watch a stock for a while, and once I find a decent opening, I enter and take the position.
